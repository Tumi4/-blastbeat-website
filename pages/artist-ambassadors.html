The Artist Ambassador programme turns the CAN Music family into champions of the Blastbeat licence. Every ambassadorship is issued personally by Robert Stephenson — and every one is tied to a live music campaign, so the artistry and the mission move together.

how it works

One artist. One campaign. One goal.

💌

1 · Robert invites you

This is not an open sign-up. Founding invitations go from Robert to artists already inside the CAN Music family — people whose work he knows.

🎤

2 · You attach a campaign

The condition of the badge: a live music campaign or main project of yours — a release, a tour, a community programme — carries the mission with it, with a sales goal attached.

🔗

3 · You get your link

A personal trackable link (blastbeat.education/r/your-name) plus a public campaign page. Every visit, every sponsor conversation it starts, is attributed to you.

💰

4 · You earn on every licence

A 25% ambassador commission on every founding licence you raise — the same ledger rate our founding ambassador earned on the first licence. Paid when the licence is paid.

Why a campaign condition? Because a badge without work behind it is just marketing. Tying every ambassadorship to a real release or project keeps the programme honest: your audience meets Blastbeat through the thing you actually made — and 75/25 of what a school cohort earns flows the same way it always has, with the climate ring-fence intact.

the kit

What every Artist Ambassador gets

  • ✉️
    A personal invitation from Robert

    Your own private onboarding page, written for you, with the terms in plain language. Nothing is public until you say yes.

  • 🔗
    A trackable personal link

    blastbeat.education/r/your-name — drop it in a bio, a video description, a post. Visits and sponsor enquiries that come through it carry your name all the way to the ledger.

  • 📊
    A public campaign page + dashboard

    Your campaign, your goal meter, your story — public and shareable. Your full private dashboard lives in the Blastbeat V2 app as it rolls out.

  • 🧠
    The credential behind you

    Every licence you help raise is a W3C Verifiable Credential — your impact is provable, not promised. See how verification works.
